Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

TravelerK Uploader ("the App") is a personal upload tool used by the operator of the TravelerK channel to publish their own short travel videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Information used

Only what the user authorizes via each platform's OAuth login: account ID, username and display name; access/refresh tokens issued by the platform; and the video file, title, caption and posting settings (privacy level, comment/duet/stitch permissions) chosen by the user. The App never stores passwords and does not collect data about other users or viewers.

Purpose

To upload and publish videos selected by the operator to the operator's own accounts and to check the posting status.

Storage and deletion

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never sent to or shared with any other server. Tokens are deleted when the connection is removed. Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's settings (e.g. TikTok Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).

Sharing

The App does not sell or share any information with third parties. Videos and post data are sent only to the platforms the user selects, through their official APIs. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
